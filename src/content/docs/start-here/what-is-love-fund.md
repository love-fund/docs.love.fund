love.fund is an everything app that fund what matters.

We facilitate pilots and campaigns that route money to the people and places we love. We give applets shared distribution and a shared go-to-market presence.  Each app solves a piece of the puzzle in its own way, builds network effects, and together the ecosystem is stronger for it - the app builds momentum for the entire ecosystem of applets.

We are exploring the following:
- Is there an opportunity to build a kickstarter for the AI era? 
- How might we reimagine collective action and fundraising for the AI/crypto era?  
- How might we disrupt the non-profit industrial complex? 
- How can we take what worked about Gitcoin into the 2030s?
- How might network effects be leveraged?  How might we build/share network effects together? How might we build shared distribution?
- What are the commanding heights in the new economy?  How can they be owned by the people whose resources they direct?
