love.fund is an ecosystem of apps that fund what matters.

We facilitate pilots and campaigns that route money to the people and places we love.

Each app solves a piece of the puzzle in its own way, and together the ecosystem is stronger for it - the app builds momentum for the entire ecosystme of apps.

We are exploring the following:
- Is there an opportunity to build a kickstarter for the contemporary era?  
- How might we reimagine collective action and fundraising for the AI/crypto era?  
- How can we take what worked about Gitcoin into the 2030s?
- How might network effects be leveraged?  How might we build/share network effects together?
- What are the commanding heights in the new economy?  How can they be owned by the people whose resources they direct?
