The plan today is to open the [call for pilots](/operational/call-for-pilots/) at launch on February 14, 2027. This page proposes going faster: run three real-money pilots in December and January, publish what happened by February 7, and make the launch about results.

## Three pilots

### 1. Fund what you love: Boulder (narrow path)

**When:** Giving Tuesday, December 1, through December 31, 2026.

- [app.love.fund](https://app.love.fund/) switches from play money to real money, for one city.
- People say what they love about Boulder, the guide drafts a fund across local nonprofits, and they give to it, follow it or fork it.
- A matching pool splits across funds by how many people back each one, so every nonprofit has a reason to rally its donors.
- **Money:** a $25k match, plus a local co-funder if we can find one in time.
- **Success looks like:** 150+ donors, $15k+ given directly, 30%+ of funds followed or forked, payouts by January 15, and a public readout.
- **What it tests:** [narrow path](/roadmap/narrow-aperture-path/) criteria 3, 5, 7, 8, 12 and 15.

### 2. People losing their jobs to AI (wide path)

**When:** January 6–31, 2027.

- A themed matching campaign for organizations that help people whose work is being automated. It's timely, mainstream, and a natural fit for people who've done well from AI.
- **Money:** a $25k match, plus an ask to AI-era funders.
- **Success looks like:** 300+ donors, $25k+ given directly, and at least one outside co-funder or press story.

### 3. A weird one (wide path)

**When:** January 6–31, 2027, with $5–10k.

- **Option A:** trust-graph allocation with a community that already knows each other ([experiments](/roadmap/experiments/) #2).
- **Option B:** a capped, paid-bounty trial with an outside developer agent ([owockibot](/operational/owockibot/)), once the conflict-of-interest questions are settled.
- **Success looks like:** the allocation runs end to end, and we write up honestly what worked, what got gamed and what we'd change.

### Then

- **February 1–7:** publish the readouts.
- **February 14:** launch with the results and open round 1.

## Money

- Matching comes from [yield on Gitcoin's matching pool](/operational/matching-pool-yield/), about $26k a month once it's put to work.
- December comes too soon for yield to build up, so this needs governance to approve a small advance (up to $60k), repaid from yield by spring.
- No rake: matching money goes only to recipients.

## How donations move

- Donors pay by card, Apple Pay or bank, and get a tax receipt. They never need a wallet.
- Gifts and the match go through a donor-advised fund charity, so love.fund never holds the money. See [payment rails](/operational/payment-rails/).
- Recipients for pilots 1 and 2 are US 501(c)(3) nonprofits.

## What has to be built

1. A real-money mode for app.love.fund: donation links per fund, a quadratic funding calculator, a "matched (estimated)" label, and a readout page.
2. Basic protection against fake accounts for match eligibility.
3. Pilot operations run by agents, following the [pilot playbook](/operational/pilot-playbook/).
4. A comms kit: landing copy, shareable stories, and a toolkit for nonprofits to rally their donors.

## Risks

- **The vote slips.** Then there's no December match. Fallback: a named sponsor for pilot 1.
- **The build runs late.** Pilot 1 moves to December 8, still inside the year.
- **Turnout is low.** The readout says so plainly. Trust is the product.
