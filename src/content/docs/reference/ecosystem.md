## Gitcoin

love.fund is a mainstream continuation of the Gitcoin mission, and it's being incubated at Gitcoin, whose governance runs on GTC.

GTC is a governance token. Holding it gives no right to love.fund's revenue, profits or assets, and nothing on this site is an offer to buy or sell GTC or any other token.

## techne

techne is an everything app for localism.

love.fund is an app that contains our fundraising experiments.

the two are complementary

1. love.fund pushes forward crowdfunding in the ai/crypto era
1. techne pushes forward to consumer distribution
1. love.fund is where techne and other aligned projects can raise.
1. techne can provide distribution/scale to love.fund experiments.
