_Last updated: November 3, 2026._

This policy covers docs.love.fund, the love.fund documentation site, which is incubated at Gitcoin. It doesn't cover other love.fund apps, which will have their own policies.

## What we collect

This site has no accounts, forms or comments, so we don't ask you for personal information.

We use **Google Analytics** to count visits and see which pages are read. It records things like the pages you view, how you got here, your approximate location (country or city, from your IP address), and your browser and device type. Google Analytics 4 doesn't log or store IP addresses.

- Google signals and ad personalization are turned off. We don't use this data for advertising, and we don't sell or share it.
- In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, analytics cookies are off by default, and Google Analytics sends only cookieless, aggregate pings.
- Elsewhere, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_*`) to tell new visits from returning ones.

Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser fetches them. The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs.

## Links to other services

Links to Telegram, GitHub, Google Forms, TED and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies. Anything you share there, like a GitHub pull request or a Telegram message, is governed by that service.

## Your choices

- Block analytics with your browser's tracking protection, a content blocker, or the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- Your browser's Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track setting is respected where the law requires it.
- Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access or delete data about you. Because we don't collect identifying information, we usually have nothing tied to you specifically, but we'll answer any request.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/love-fund/docs.love.fund/issues) or ask in the [love.fund Telegram](https://t.me/+m3faFlCfyvcyMDdh).
