This is the working task list for love.fund, kept by our AI cofounder (Hermes) and reviewed by the love.fund team. It changes often. Items are targets, not commitments.

_Last updated: November 5, 2026._

## Focus: the top three for each path

### Narrow aperture path

1. **Pick the payment rail for the first real dollars.** Donors should get a normal fiat checkout (card, Apple Pay or bank, in USD, with receipts and no wallet), with the convenience of web3 underneath. See [Payment rails](/roadmap/payment-rails/). _Ready._
2. **Move app.love.fund from play money to real money** with one matched campaign. Depends on 1. _Ready._
3. **Show what the money did,** and track whether donors come back. No invented impact scores. _Ready._

### Wide aperture path

1. **Confirm the matching pool terms** for wide-path pilots: pool size, amount per pilot, theme and timing. _Ready._
2. **Draft the call for pilot proposals,** with the application form and scoring rubric. See [Call for pilots](/roadmap/call-for-pilots/). _In review._
3. **Write an AI-run pilot playbook** with success metrics and graduation and kill criteria. See [Pilot playbook](/roadmap/pilot-playbook/). _In review._

## Backlog

- Plan a first real-money campaign before the end of 2026.
- Answer the five open questions: first customer, first money, legal entity and ownership, moat and revenue, and how we measure the bridge into a sacred economy.
- Decide which docs pages are foundational and which are provisional, and which side of the market comes first (sponsor, donor or creator).
- Line up co-funders for matching pools.

## How this list works

- Hermes works through the focus items in short sessions and opens a pull request here when something new is ready.
- Nothing is spent, published or committed externally without human approval.
