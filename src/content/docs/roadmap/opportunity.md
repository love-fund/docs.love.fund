## The opportunity: build the rails that allocate capital in the AI economy

The core insight of [The Commanding Heights](https://commanding-heights.com) is that the biggest fortunes won't necessarily come from creating intelligence, but from controlling how its economic value flows.

The emerging commanding height in finance isn't just payments or stablecoins. It's the infrastructure that decides who gets capital, on what terms, and who owns the resulting upside.

## Five potential commanding heights in traditional finance

| Infrastructure           | What it controls                      | Opportunity for Techne / Love Fund                           |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1. Capital discovery     | Who gets seen and funded              | Open social graph + funding recommendations                  |
| 2. Capital allocation    | Who receives money and why            | QF, trust graphs, AI funding agents                          |
| 3. Underwriting          | Who is creditworthy or investable     | Portable reputation and impact history                       |
| 4. Capital formation     | How communities pool and invest money | Community funding pools, revenue-based financing             |
| 5. Ownership and returns | Who captures future wealth            | Cooperative equity, shared upside, regenerative reinvestment |


## Commanding heights of a more sacred economy

Owning the commanding heights of the commodified, transactional global economy isn't the end goal. love.fund is also focused on building a [sacred economy](/ontology/dialectics/sacred-global-economy/): one where the economy serves what people hold sacred, like relationships, community, and the living world.

So we want to build two sets of rails:

- **Rails for the old economy.** The commanding heights above: capital discovery, allocation, underwriting, formation, and ownership. We run them at first, and progressively decentralize them to the people who use them.
- **Rails for a more sacred, relational economy.** Infrastructure where value flows through trust and relationships instead of extraction, and where what matters gets funded even when it can't be priced.

The first set is the bridge to the second. We meet people where they are, in the economy most of the world runs on, and use those rails to move them, and their capital, into a new sacred economy. (See [Towards a more sacred economy](/ontology/dialectics/sacred-global-economy/#towards-a-more-sacred-economy).)

### Seven rails for a sacred economy

Working backwards from what we've written about a sacred economy, the rails follow its journey: shield first, then weave, then meet the global economy on your own terms.

| Rail | What it does | Where it comes from | Closest trad commanding height |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Shield** | Patient, no-strings, multi-year capital that buys a community time and room, so it doesn't have to sell out to survive | [Sacred x Global](/ontology/dialectics/sacred-global-economy/), [Short x Long-term](/ontology/dialectics/short-long-term/) | Capital formation |
| **2. Weave** | The trust fabric: vouching, reputation built over time, many kinds of evidence. Dreams, community plans and indigenous knowledge only become an economy when braided together | [Sacred x Global](/ontology/dialectics/sacred-global-economy/), [Trust x Verification](/ontology/dialectics/trust-verification/) | Discovery + underwriting |
| **3. Gift** | Money that keeps moving toward need and love, passed forward instead of paid back, with enough accounting to stay fair | [Gift x Exchange](/ontology/dialectics/gift-exchange/) | Allocation (impact) |
| **4. Meaning** | Communities name what matters in their own words first.  | [Measurement x Meaning](/ontology/dialectics/measurement-meaning/) | None: the old economy uses price |
| **5. Boundary** | A governed commons: the right to say no, protection from capture, share-alike licenses, and things kept outside the market entirely | [Openness x Boundaries](/ontology/dialectics/openness-boundaries/), [Sovereignty x Interdependence](/ontology/dialectics/sovereignty-interdependence/) | Ownership, inverted: protecting instead of capturing |
| **6. Membrane** | The interface where outside money enters on the community's terms and gets translated into its values, when the community decides it's ready | [Sacred x Global](/ontology/dialectics/sacred-global-economy/), [Local x Global](/ontology/dialectics/local-global/) | None |
| **7. Federation** | Communities connecting directly to each other, not only through global intermediaries | [Local x Global](/ontology/dialectics/local-global/), [Sovereignty x Interdependence](/ontology/dialectics/sovereignty-interdependence/) | None |

A few things stand out:

- **The rails don't map one-to-one.** Shield, weave and gift roughly correspond to the trad commanding heights. Meaning, membrane and federation have no equivalent, because the commodified economy doesn't need them. 
- **Commanding heights control chokepoints. Sacred rails are membranes and circulation.** You win the old economy by owning the bottleneck. A sacred economy works when value circulates and each community holds its own boundary.
- **The bridge runs through the membrane.** The rails we run in the commodified economy feed money into the membrane, which translates it into the sacred economy. As we progressively decentralize, the membrane is the part we most need to hand to communities.
