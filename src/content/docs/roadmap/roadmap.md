**TLDR:** fund what people love, at a scale and quality that isn't possible today.

How: a crowdfunding platform that builds the rails that allocate capital in the AI economy

Why: build the commanding height(s) of the next era, then distribute ownership to the people who use them 

## Principles

- **We are AI-native.** Instead of building a large team, we build and support our systems with AI.
- **Linear inputs, exponential outputs.** We favor work that compounds through network effects: each new funder, fund and cause makes love.fund more useful to the next one.
- **We are techne-adjacent.** We build alongside [Techne Foundation](https://technefoundation.org).

## What we're building

**What:** a new crowdfunding everything app that funds what we love 10x better than is possible right now.

**Why:** fund what you love, and see more of what you love in the world.

**Opportunity:** build a Kickstarter for the AI/crypto era, and build network effects in the neocrowdfunding space. AI and crypto open new design space in collective action, network participation and crowdfunding civic participation.

People don't switch for something 10% better. They switch for something 10x better, so that's what we have to build.

## Three eras

**North star:** become the Kickstarter / GoFundMe of the AI/crypto era.

We get there in three eras:

1. **Pilots (starting now):** allocation campaigns that prove what works 10x better than existing solutions in the frontier.
2. **Products (next 12-24 months):** repeatably useful tools with month-over-month product-led growth.
3. **Platform (next 36 months):** we systematically launch banger products and create a category-defining product suite, and distribution grows exponentially.

## Narrow and wide aperture paths

The narrow path and the wide path are two complementary apertures for realizing our vision.

- **The [wide aperture path](/roadmap/wide-aperture-path/)** is where we engage our community and inspire each other to dream big and try weird, [experimental](/roadmap/experiments/) things. Think of it as decentralized R&D.
- **The [narrow aperture path](/roadmap/narrow-aperture-path/)** is a narrow and rigorous path for the future of funding, prefigured from our experience and a priori thinking.

Over time, we expect that:

- the narrow path will develop into a platform that becomes the centerpiece of love.fund.
- the wide path will become a plugin ecosystem of bespoke funding networks.
- we will explore the possibility of AI/crypto-based collective action movements that create the neocrowdfunding era.

### Possible paths forward

Kevin is deciding between:

1. Doing just the narrow path.
2. Doing the narrow path and the wide path together.
