The narrow path is a narrow and rigorous path for the future of funding, prefigured from our experience and a priori thinking. Over time, we expect it to develop into a platform that becomes the centerpiece of love.fund.

**Demo:** [app.love.fund](http://app.love.fund/)

## Criteria

Letters credit the contributor behind each criterion (D, E and K).

### Intent

1. Help people tell love from wants, preferences and addictions, using the deathbed test. (D)
2. Fund specific people and places, and hold the strategy loosely. (D)
3. Go from plain-English intent to a fund you can edit, at any level from block to planet. (K)
4. Make money flow like metta: unconditional, widening and practiced, more than transacted. (D)

### Giving

5. Fund the missing middle: visible local impact, pooled enough to matter. (E)
6. Keep donors sovereign, with overridable defaults like 85% to the project and 15% upstream, shown to the donor before they give. (E)
7. Let small donors ride with big ones through follow, copy and fork. (K)
8. Never charge the small donor, or let fees make a small gift pointless. (E, K)
9. Cover all six resources, including attention and creativity as well as money. (D)

### Delivery

10. Settle the legal structure (nonprofit, fiscal sponsor or company) before taking donations, and tell donors plainly whether a gift is tax-deductible. (E)
11. Make spending auditable. (E)
12. Show what the money did, with no invented impact scores. (K)
13. Use escrow and bounties for projects that don't exist yet, through a licensed provider. (E)
14. Keep the human switchboard temporary, with a written plan to automate it. (E, K)

### Growth and trust

15. Use matching as the driver. (E)
16. Build network effects on public-by-default funds, with visible credit for authors. (K)
17. Give each person a scorecard of what they love against where their time and money go. (D)
18. Show where the money goes, and earn momentum first, then back it with solid accounting. (E)
19. Lead with the cause, not the technology, and be plain about what people get: backers fund work, they don't invest in it, and they get no financial return. Disclose when crypto is involved. (E, K)

### Revenue

20. Target 10% of platform activity as revenue, from fees that are disclosed before anyone gives and never charged to small donors (see 8).

### Operations

21. **AI-forward.** The team is Kevin and 1-2 other people, and agents otherwise.
22. **Exploring collaboration with Techne Foundation.** The Techne app could give us distribution, and we could give communities funding superpowers.
23. **Exploring collaboration with Tech Coop,** including possible pilots in Boulder.
24. **Interoperable with other apps** via atproto and plugin infrastructure.

## The funding graph

The funding graph is the core IP that builds over time. It connects three things:

- **Funders** (demand)
- **Intent**
