The wide path is where we engage our community and inspire each other to dream big and try weird, experimental things. Think of it as decentralized R&D. Over time, we expect it to become a plugin ecosystem of bespoke funding networks.

We take the best parts of GG24 (domain-driven allocations) and techne (localism focus), and become a domain-driven funder of neocrowdfunding pilots for localism.

We facilitate pilots and campaigns that route money to the people and places doing the work, and we lend them our name, software and distribution so they don't have to build those from scratch.

It starts as facilitation: someone brings a pilot funding idea, we screen for the best ones, then run it. The ones that work graduate into ongoing love.fund campaigns.

Our goals:

- Attract world-class developers and mechanism designers to love.fund.
- Explore [new experiments](/roadmap/experiments/) in funding what matters.
- Build a plugin, platform and liquidity layer that adds value to the [narrow path](/roadmap/narrow-aperture-path/).

## Pilots (short term)

Before we say yes to a pilot, we want answers to these:

- **Where's the money coming from?** A named funder, a matching pool, or donors we can actually reach.
- **Where's the audience coming from?** Who shows up to give, apply or vote, and through which channel.
- **What are we trying to accomplish?** One outcome we can check at the end, so we know whether to keep going.
- **What's the theory of change?** Why we can help when others can't or won't.

If a pilot can't answer the first one, it isn't ready.

## Campaigns (long term)

A pilot becomes a campaign when it can carry these:

- **love.fund branded**, or co-branded with the partner.
- **A sustainable fee model**, disclosed to everyone before they give, so campaigns cover their own costs instead of draining the platform.
- **Good software** that donors and recipients actually enjoy using.
- **Good operations:** payouts land on time, reporting is clean, and nobody's chasing a spreadsheet.

It's powerful enough that we want to build it into the love.fund platform or the techne everything app as the flagship of our early "funding" section.
