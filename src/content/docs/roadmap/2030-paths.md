It's 2030. How did it go?

:::note
These are scenarios to think with, not predictions or promises. Most likely, 2030 is some mix of them. None of them describes a return for GTC holders or anyone else.
:::

## NGMI

love.fund never seriuosly gets past pilots. The campaigns don't pay their way, donors don't come back after the first round, and the brand fades into the long list of crypto funding experiments that didn't make it. Gitcoin's lessons get written up one more time, and someone else builds the Kickstarter for the AI era.

This is the path the [lessons in the track record](/start-here/track-record/#lessons) are there to avoid: size the team to the market, make campaigns pay their way, and ship.

## WGMI: the trad economy path and the sacred economy path

love.fund runs rails in the economy most of the world already uses, and those rails carry people and their capital into a [more sacred economy](/ontology/dialectics/sacred-global-economy/). Both paths are live, and the first one funds the second.

## WGMI: a thriving atproto ecosystem + one app

The [atproto ecosystem](/roadmap/architecture/) thrives, and love.fund is the one app everyone uses to fund things in it. The data is open, but the best way to act on it is ours.

## WGMI: a thriving atproto ecosystem + many apps

The ecosystem thrives, and love.fund is a family of funding apps: a gift circle here, a matching campaign there, a local venture fund somewhere else, all reading and writing the same graph. No single app wins. The network does.

## WGMI: the commanding heights to the sacred economy

love.fund helps build the [commanding heights of the AI economy](/roadmap/opportunity/) and turns them into a funnel into the sacred economy, then progressively hands ownership of those rails to the people who use them.

The moat isn't the AI allocation algorithm. It's the compounding network of trust, capital relationships, repayment history, impact and recurring funding flows.

## WGMI: a capital allocation evolutionary environment

love.fund becomes an [evolutionary arena for capital allocation](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/temp-check-gitcoin-3-3-3-3-an-evolutionary-arena-for-capital-allocation/22680): many mechanisms compete on real-world outcomes, and the best ones get funding, distribution and visibility. The [design space](/roadmap/design-space/) gets searched on purpose instead of by accident.

## WGMI: Techne's everything app for localists sees exponential usage

Techne launches an everything app for localists, and usage grows exponentially.

- love.fund is on an exponential curve too. People who made fortunes in AI are starting to use it, and so are everyday people, through the Techne app.
